Frequently asked questions

How does IPMe detect my IP address?

IPMe queries a third-party geolocation API from your device. No data passes through any intermediary server.

Does IPMe collect any data?

No. IPMe has no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking. Everything stays on your Mac. Read our Privacy Policy.

What's included in IPMe Pro?

IPMe Pro ($3.99, one-time purchase) unlocks Network Pulse (latency, jitter, packet loss), DNS Leak Test, IP History, smart notifications, and more.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?

Open IPMe, click the gear icon, and your purchase will be restored automatically through your Apple ID.
